Study guide

Study Notes

A single reference page for the most common terms, patterns, and signals you should remember across the rest of the site.

Core concepts

  • ATT&CK: Adversary behavior mapped to tactics and techniques.
  • Tactic: The attacker’s goal, such as initial access, persistence, or exfiltration.
  • Technique: The specific method used to achieve that goal.
  • Kill chain: A staged model showing how intrusions typically progress.
  • Detection engineering: Building sensor logic and analytics around observable malicious behavior.
  • Defense-in-depth: Multiple overlapping layers of control to reduce risk and contain failures.

High-yield signal checklist

  • Unexpected logins from new geographies or off-hours hours.
  • PowerShell or script execution from office documents or browsers.
  • Scheduled tasks, services, registry autoruns, or persistence artifacts.
  • Rare DNS lookups to high-entropy domains or suspicious TLDs.
  • Mass file access, archive creation, or large outbound transfers.
  • Unauthorized remote logins or privilege escalation events.

Useful questions to ask

  • What is the user, host, and asset owner?
  • Is this expected behavior or a deviation from a known baseline?
  • What is the likely tactic and stage of the intrusion?
  • What telemetry confirms or rejects the hypothesis?
  • What is the immediate containment priority?
