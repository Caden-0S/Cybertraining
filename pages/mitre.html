Frameworks

MITRE

MITRE ATT&CK is a knowledge base of adversary behavior mapped to tactics and techniques. It is widely used to classify attack activity, support detections, and guide incident response and threat hunting.

Core concepts

ATT&CK Tactics

Tactics describe the goal of the attacker at a given point, such as initial access, persistence, privilege escalation, discovery, or exfiltration.

ATT&CK Techniques

Techniques are the concrete behavior used to achieve a tactic, such as phishing, credential dumping, scheduled task abuse, or remote service exploitation.

ATT&CK Matrices

The framework is organized by platform and scope, including enterprise, cloud, mobile, and industrial control systems.

Why it matters

Using ATT&CK lets analysts communicate clearly, map detections to adversary behavior, and build hunting and detection content around real-world attack chains.

My notes

Record technique mappings, detection ideas, and interview-ready explanations.

Key takeaways

  • Tactics answer “why?”
  • Techniques answer “how?”
  • ATT&CK is useful for both detection engineering and interviews
  • Good analysts map alerts to the broader attack lifecycle.
