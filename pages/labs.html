Hands-on investigation

Labs

Work through short analyst scenarios. Identify the behavior, map it to a framework, and explain what evidence would confirm your hypothesis.

Lab 1: Suspicious login sequence

A finance user signs in from a new country at 02:00. Minutes later, PowerShell runs and a scheduled task appears. Identify the likely access, execution, and persistence behaviors.

  • Which identity and endpoint logs would you check first?
  • What would distinguish a compromised account from a false positive?
  • Which containment action should happen first?

Lab 2: DNS beaconing

An endpoint resolves high-entropy domains at a regular interval and makes rare outbound HTTPS connections. Investigate whether the pattern suggests command and control.

  • Compare interval, jitter, volume, and domain age.
  • Correlate DNS, proxy, and endpoint process telemetry.
  • Record the indicators you would block or hunt.

Lab notes

Keep a short investigation record for each scenario, including evidence, hypotheses, and next actions.
