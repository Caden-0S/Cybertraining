Interview preparation

Practice

Turn knowledge into concise analyst answers. Use these prompts to rehearse how you would investigate, communicate, and recommend action.

Explain the alert

Describe how you would triage a suspicious PowerShell alert from first signal to confirmed or dismissed incident.

  • What context would you gather?
  • Which related events would you correlate?
  • How would you communicate confidence and impact?

Map the behavior

Choose one behavior from the topic pages and explain its tactic, technique, evidence sources, and likely defensive control.

  • State the attacker objective in plain language.
  • Name the telemetry that supports the mapping.
  • Suggest one prevention and one detection measure.

Draft interview answers, questions to revisit, and examples you want to explain more clearly.
